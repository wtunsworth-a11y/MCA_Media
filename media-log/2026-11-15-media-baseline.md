## Most active recent source (2026): Landscape Alliance / CIFOR-ICRAF *Forests News*

[Managalas Conservation Area tag page](https://www.forestsnews.org/tag/managalas-conservation-area)

- [Mapping biodiversity on the Managalas Plateau](https://sutpp.cifor-icraf.org/mapping-biodiversity-on-papua-new-guineas-managalas-plateau/) (June 2026): local youth run camera traps and acoustic monitors; survey due to finish end of 2026.
- [Social media workshop shows digital gaps](https://www.forestsnews.org/163949/smartphone-training-digital-access-managalas) (October 2026): limited smartphone access, connectivity and digital confidence, which helps explain the low social-media activity from the plateau itself.
- [Magic beans: Can Managalas capitalize on coffee?](https://www.forestsnews.org/155879/managalas-coffee-papua-new-guinea-livelihoods) (February 2026).
- Also in 2026: cocoa revival, masoi bark harvesting, house-building trees, and field surveys suggesting a possible Birdwing butterfly decline.
- Background: [Making Managalas](https://www.cifor-icraf.org/knowledge/news/91762/); [EU-FCCB Managalas and Oro Province brochure (PDF)](https://www.landscapealliance.org/managalas-oro-province-project/wp-content/uploads/sites/57/2024/11/Brochure-MOPP_web.pdf).

## Things to watch

- **Size:** Sources disagree. Most say about 360,000 ha (3,600 km²); Wikipedia gives 214,696 ha.
- **Mining:** Not specifically excluded in the management plan. A recurring concern, though no recent news on it was found.
- **No results from:** Post-Courier, FM100, NBC.
