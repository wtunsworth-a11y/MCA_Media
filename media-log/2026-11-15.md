# Later run — 2026-11-15 (15:00 PNG time)

## Issues / criticism

**No new criticism found.** One clarification to an earlier item:

- **The Omie "Managalas carbon scheme" complaint is a reader comment from 2021, not a press release.** [PNG Bulletin, 24 Aug 2021](https://thepngbulletin.com/?p=6864). The article itself is a government press release on the launch of the Managalas Forest Carbon Pilot Project. The complaint is the only comment under it, posted 27 Aug 2021 by Gilphas Areura. He says Omie Tribe land was included in the Managalas plateau for conservation and carbon trading "without proper land mediation and signing", without clan chiefs' approval or ILG vetting, and calls this unlawful. He asks the PM's carbon trading office and the pilot project to settle it "amicably". No reply is shown. It is aimed at the 2021 pilot under PM Marape, not at EU-FCCB or Landscape Alliance. Low urgency, but it remains a land-boundary grievance on the public record. *Page opened.*
