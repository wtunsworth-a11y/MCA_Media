- **PNG Nature Pact workshop, APEC Haus, about 23 June 2026: PM Marape cited Managalas as the model.** According to search summaries of the [PM's office release](https://www.pmnec.gov.pg/prime-minister-marape-launches-inaugural-png-nature-pact-workshop-calls-for-global-partnership-to-protect-forests-oceans-and-biodiversity/) and [Islands Business: "PNG nature pact to turn forest protection into local income"](https://islandsbusiness.com/news-break/png-nature-pact-to-turn-forest-protection-into-local-income/), Marape said nature must earn income for customary landowners, "not just profits for loggers and carbon traders". He warned against "poorly regulated carbon trading" and pointed to the Managalas initiative in Oro as the approach the government wants to promote.
