## Other new mentions

- [Forests News, 12 Oct 2026: "How forest trees provide timber for homes in Papua New Guinea"](https://www.forestsnews.org/164329/managalas-plateau-housing-timber-trees). Monica Evans writes up forester Swancy Saiva's 2025 survey of 224 households in 48 villages. It found most house timber comes from old gardens and secondary fallow forest, and the results will guide two community nurseries. The piece notes concerns: young people are losing knowledge of tree species, and timber supply is uncertain as the population grows. It criticises nobody and has no reader comments.
